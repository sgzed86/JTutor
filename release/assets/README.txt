Irodori materials (required — not included in this package)
==========================================================

Japan Foundation copyright. Download your own copies from the official site:
https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/

Place files here:

  assets\
    irodori_starter.pdf          (Starter textbook)
    Grammar_Worksheets_X.pdf     (Starter grammar)
    Elementary1.pdf              (Elementary 1 textbook — optional)
    Grammar_Elementary_1.pdf     (Elementary 1 grammar — optional)
    audio\
      X_*.mp3                    (Starter CD audio)
      Y_*.mp3                    (Elementary 1 CD audio)

Audio filenames look like:
  X_[01-01]_kiku.mp3
  Y_[01-01]_kaiwa1.mp3

After copying files, run START.bat again.
